How to Use

Browse — Scroll through the glass cards to explore word roots. Use the A–Z bar to filter by first letter.

Search — Type a root, meaning, or any word from the book. If the word belongs to a root, that root's card will appear with a match highlight.

Click a card — See the root's full notes, example words, and more vocabulary from the book. Click any word to expand its definition, pronunciation (US & UK IPA), example sentence, and usage notes.

Add entries — As you study Merriam-Webster's Vocabulary Builder, tell Claude which roots you've learned. Each card is built from the book's full word data.

About

This notebook was built to accompany the study of Merriam-Webster's Vocabulary Builder (Second Edition). Its goal is to make the book's 249 word roots and 1,199 vocabulary words browsable, searchable, and deeply explorable.

The design draws from the AndDream brand language: deep teal and warm white, liquid glass cards with live specular highlights, and macOS-inspired interface details. Every word detail — definition, example sentence, usage notes, and both US & UK IPA pronunciations — is sourced from the book or verified online.

Built by Andrew. Typography: Cochin, Snell Roundhand, Big Caslon, and Inter. Deployed on GitHub Pages. Continuously growing, one root at a time.
