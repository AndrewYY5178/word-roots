How to Use

Browse — Scroll through the glass cards to explore word roots. Filter by first letter (A–Z) or by Unit (1–30) using the bars below the search box. Click Quizzes / Review Quizzes to access interactive exercises.

Search — Type a root, meaning, or any vocabulary word from Merriam-Webster's Vocabulary Builder. If the word belongs to a root, that root's card will appear with a match highlight.

Click a card — See the root's full notes, example words, and more vocabulary. Click any word to expand its definition, pronunciation (US & UK IPA), example sentence, and usage notes.

Take a quiz — Each quiz covers two roots with 5 question types: Synonym, Analogy, Indicate (Same/Different), Match, and Fill-in-the-blank. Click an answer for instant feedback (green = correct, red = wrong), or use Check All for match and fill sections. Use ← Back to return to the quiz list.

Annotate words — Select any word or phrase in a quiz or card detail by dragging your cursor over it. A bubble will appear where you can add a Chinese annotation. Annotated words are highlighted in gold everywhere they appear. Hover over them to see your notes. Select an already-annotated word to edit or delete it.

Cloud sync — Annotations are saved in your browser instantly, and — once you set a sync passphrase via Sync setup in the footer — automatically backed up to the cloud so they survive clearing your browser and appear on every device. On a new device, open Sync setup and enter the same passphrase to pull them in.

Word look-up — Search any word from the book to instantly find its root card. Matching words are highlighted in green on the card.

About

This notebook was built to accompany the study of Merriam-Webster's Vocabulary Builder (Second Edition). Its goal is to make the book's 249 word roots and 1,199 vocabulary words browsable, searchable, and deeply explorable.

The design draws from the AndDream brand language: deep teal and warm white, liquid glass cards with live specular highlights, and macOS-inspired interface details. Every word detail — definition, example sentence, usage notes, and both US & UK IPA pronunciations — is sourced from the book or verified online.

Built by Andrew. Typography: Cochin, Snell Roundhand, Big Caslon, and Inter. Deployed on GitHub Pages. Continuously growing, one root at a time.
