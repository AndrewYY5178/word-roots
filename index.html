How to Use

Browse — Scroll through the glass cards to explore word roots. Filter by first letter (A–Z) or by Unit (1–30) using the bars below the search box. Click Quizzes / Review Quizzes to access interactive exercises.

Search — Type a root, meaning, or any vocabulary word from Merriam-Webster's Vocabulary Builder. If the word belongs to a root, that root's card will appear with a match highlight.

Click a card — See the root's full notes, example words, and more vocabulary. Click any word to expand its definition, pronunciation (US & UK IPA), example sentence, and usage notes. At the bottom, Practice buttons jump straight into the quizzes and unit review that cover this root.

Take a quiz — Each quiz covers two roots with 5 question types: Synonym, Analogy, Indicate (Same/Different), Match, and Fill-in-the-blank. Click an answer for instant feedback (green = correct, red = wrong), or use Check All for match and fill sections. Use ← Back to return to the quiz list, or the root buttons in the top-right to jump to a covered root's card.

Flashcards — A quick daily drill of every word you've learned. Switch between EN → 中 (see English, recall Chinese) and 中 → EN. One side is blurred — tap it to reveal the answer. Use the shuffle button to randomize the order so you learn the meanings, not the positions.

Annotate words — Add your own Chinese notes to unfamiliar words. Open a card or start a quiz first (annotation works inside those). On a computer, drag your cursor across a word or phrase to select it. On a phone, long-press a word and drag the handles to extend the selection — your phone's own Copy / Look-Up menu may appear above it, so just use the gold bubble that pops up below the selection. Type your note and tap Save to get a gold note, or leave the box blank and tap Save to just highlight the word (mint, no note). Hover (computer) or tap (phone) a gold word to read its note, and select any highlighted word again to edit, re-color, or delete it.

Cloud sync & Sync setup — Notes are saved in your browser instantly and stay after reloading. To back them up and share them across devices, scroll down to the footer, tap Sync setup, enter your sync passphrase, and tap Save. Do this once on every device and browser (phone and computer), using the same passphrase each time. The footer shows Synced when a backup succeeds, or Saved locally if the sync server can't be reached — either way your notes are safe on that device.

Word look-up — Search any word from the book to instantly find its root card. Matching words are highlighted in green on the card.

About

This notebook was built to accompany the study of Merriam-Webster's Vocabulary Builder (Second Edition). Its goal is to make the book's 249 word roots and 1,199 vocabulary words browsable, searchable, and deeply explorable.

The design draws from the AndDream brand language: deep teal and warm white, liquid glass cards with live specular highlights, and macOS-inspired interface details. Every word detail — definition, example sentence, usage notes, and both US & UK IPA pronunciations — is sourced from the book or verified online.

Built by Andrew. Typography: Cochin, Snell Roundhand, Big Caslon, and Inter. Deployed on GitHub Pages. Continuously growing, one root at a time.
